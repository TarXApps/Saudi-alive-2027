Six pillars / The next generation of living

Six sectors shaping better living.

Each pillar brings together the companies that supply, activate and operate modern communities, and the people who buy from them.

Six pillars

Smart Living & Health

01 / 06 · Live Connected

Smart Living & Developments

New developments need more than buildings: technology-enabled homes, complete neighbourhoods, great public spaces and effective community operations.

Who will exhibit

Master developers; property groups; PropTech; architects; smart-home systems; community service providers.

Who will visit

Property and asset owners; municipalities; investors; facilities managers; master-planning and procurement teams.

02 / 06 · Live Well

Modern Healthcare & Wellbeing

Prevention, accessible care, wellness and healthier neighbourhoods create opportunities across facilities, technology and community services.

Who will exhibit

Healthcare providers; digital health; medical technology; wellness brands; sports and active-living operators.

Who will visit

Health groups; hospital operators; employers; insurers; developers; institutional and facilities buyers.

Six pillars

Public Mobility & Education

03 / 06 · Live in Motion

Public & Community Mobility

Connected public transport, walkable access and accessible movement are essential to more liveable cities and destinations.

Who will exhibit

Public transport technology; transit systems; shared mobility; accessibility; wayfinding; last-mile solutions.

Who will visit

Transport and municipal authorities; developers; public-transport operators; destination and infrastructure teams.

04 / 06 · Live & Learn

Future Education & Skills

Growing cities and new industries need adaptable learning environments, digital education and skills that keep pace with demand.

Who will exhibit

Universities; K-12 providers; EdTech; vocational academies; learning spaces; professional-training platforms.

Who will visit

Education authorities; school groups; universities; employers; HR leaders; developers of educational assets.

Six pillars

Culture & Sustainability

05 / 06 · Live Inspired

Culture, Heritage & Experiences

Culture becomes economic value when heritage, creative industries and memorable experiences are brought into everyday life.

Who will exhibit

Cultural institutions; museums; heritage technology; arts and design; experiential agencies; venue operators.

Who will visit

Cultural bodies; destination developers; tourism operators; venues; hospitality groups; creative investors.

06 / 06 · Live Sustainably

Resilient, Low-Impact Communities

Sustainable living depends on green buildings, circular systems, water efficiency and greener, more resilient public spaces.

Who will exhibit

Green-building solutions; water and energy tech; environmental design; recycling; landscape and smart infrastructure.

Who will visit

Developers; municipalities; utilities; sustainability directors; asset and facility operators; institutional buyers.
