About the event · 1–3 June 2027

Where better living meets business opportunity.

The Quality of Life, Development & Investment Exhibition

Saudi Arabia is creating new opportunities for everyday life.

Communities, services and experiences are making daily life healthier, smarter and more connected. LIVNEXT Saudi 2027 connects developers, government stakeholders, operators, investors, service providers and brands across eight quality of life sectors.

Across the exhibition and summit, LIVNEXT supports product visibility, industry dialogue, business networking and practical partnership opportunities. Organised by Connect Spot Exhibitions at RICEC, Riyadh, 1–3 June 2027.
