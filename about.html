About the event

The next generation of living is also the next generation of opportunity.

The event in one sentence

A focused B2B exhibition and summit.

LIVNEXT Saudi is a focused B2B exhibition and summit connecting developers, asset owners, operators and public-sector stakeholders with the solutions that make places healthier, more connected, more memorable and more sustainable.

Organised by Connect Spot Exhibitions in Riyadh, in 2027.
